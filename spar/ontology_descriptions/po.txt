#description The **_Pattern Ontology (PO)_** is an OWL 2 DL ontology that formally defines patterns for segmenting a document into atomic components, in order to be manipulated independently and re-flowed in different contexts.

Structural patterns allow representing document elements (such as sections, paragraphs, lists, metadata, and tables) through abstract, reusable primitives independent of specific markup syntaxes.
