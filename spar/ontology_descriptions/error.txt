#description The **_Error Ontology_** is an unit test that allow to produce an inconsistent model if a particular (and incorrect) situation happens.

It works by means of a data property `error:hasError` that denies its usage for ant resource. 
In fact, by defining its domain as "all those resource that do not have any `hasErrorDescription` assertion", a resource that asserts having an error makes the ontology inconsistent.
